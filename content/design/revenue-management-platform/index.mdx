## Overview

The platform processes billions in transaction volume, requiring finance teams to reconcile payments across multiple gateways, currencies, and bank accounts. The manual process was slow, error-prone, and scaling poorly.

## Challenge

Reconciliation involved exporting CSV files from three separate systems, manually matching transactions in spreadsheets, and investigating discrepancies through email chains. A single month-end close could take two weeks.

## My Approach

I worked alongside finance operators to understand their daily workflows, then mapped the ideal automated flow. The key insight was that most discrepancies follow predictable patterns that can be resolved automatically.

## Solution

The platform automatically matches transactions across sources, flags exceptions with context, and provides a clear interface for resolving unmatched items. Real-time dashboards give leadership visibility into revenue health.

## Impact

Month-end close dropped from two weeks to three days. Error rates in reconciliation fell by 80%, and the finance team could focus on analysis instead of data entry.
