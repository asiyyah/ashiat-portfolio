## Overview

The payment platform enables businesses to send and receive payments across 50+ currencies. The core challenge was making complex financial operations feel straightforward.

## Challenge

Finance teams needed to manage multi-currency accounts, track settlement timelines, and reconcile transactions — all while adhering to regulatory requirements in multiple jurisdictions.

## My Approach

I mapped the entire payment lifecycle from initiation to settlement, identifying pain points at each stage. This systems-level view revealed opportunities to consolidate steps and automate manual processes.

## Solution

The platform uses a conversation-style flow for payment creation, progressive disclosure for advanced settings, and a unified dashboard for monitoring all transactions across currencies.

## Impact

Transaction completion improved 25%, and the time finance teams spent on reconciliation dropped by nearly a third.
