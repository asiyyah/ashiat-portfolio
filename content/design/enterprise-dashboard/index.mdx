## Overview

The enterprise dashboard serves financial analysts who monitor real-time risk metrics across multiple portfolios. The original interface had grown organically, resulting in cognitive overload and slow decision-making.

## Challenge

Users had to navigate through 15+ screens to complete what should have been a three-click workflow. Key metrics were buried under nested menus, and the absence of consistent design patterns made learning the system unnecessarily difficult.

## My Approach

I started by shadowing users during their daily workflows. This revealed that analysts switch between monitoring mode and investigation mode throughout the day — a distinction the original design ignored.

## Solution

The new design separates monitoring from investigation. The primary view shows critical metrics with configurable thresholds and alerts. Clicking through leads to progressively detailed views without losing context.

## Impact

The redesign reduced the average time to identify and act on risk events from 12 minutes to under 3 minutes.
