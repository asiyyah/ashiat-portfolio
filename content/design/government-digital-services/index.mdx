## Overview

Citizens applying for government services faced a maze of paper forms, duplicate data entry, and inconsistent experiences across departments. The goal was to create a unified, accessible digital experience.

## Challenge

Each government department had its own forms, processes, and digital systems. Citizens had to re-enter the same information multiple times, and the language used was often bureaucratic and confusing.

## My Approach

I began with inclusive research sessions involving citizens with varying levels of digital literacy, including those with disabilities. This revealed that clarity and simplicity mattered more than visual polish.

## Solution

The platform uses plain-language content, a consistent component library meeting WCAG AA standards, and a unified application that shares data across departments so citizens never enter the same information twice.

## Impact

Form completion rates increased significantly, error rates dropped, and the platform now serves over 2 million citizens across 12 government services.
