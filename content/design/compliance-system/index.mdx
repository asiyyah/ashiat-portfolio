## Overview

A financial institution managing multiple regulatory frameworks needed a unified system to track compliance obligations, collect evidence, and prepare for audits.

## Challenge

Compliance officers spent weeks preparing for each audit, manually gathering evidence from disparate systems. The process was reactive, stressful, and prone to oversight.

## My Approach

I conducted workshops with compliance teams, auditors, and legal counsel to understand the full regulatory lifecycle. This revealed that most compliance work is about preparation, not reaction.

## Solution

The platform shifts compliance from reactive to proactive. Automated evidence collection runs continuously, dashboards show regulatory posture in real time, and audit preparation becomes a one-click export.

## Impact

The system transformed audit readiness from a quarterly scramble to a continuous state of preparedness.
